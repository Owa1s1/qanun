Questions

Which plan should I choose?

Students preparing for MBBS, BDS, FCPS Part 1 or PGMI need the Student plan. Doctors who also want clinical search, the daily brief and bedside tools should choose Clinician.

How do I pay?

On the Android app, through Google Play. For the computer apps and the web, pay by Easypaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, SadaPay or bank transfer: message us on WhatsApp and we activate your plan.

Can I use one plan on several devices?

A plan is for one person, on one computer and one phone or tablet, plus the web. One device can be in use at a time.

Does my plan renew by itself?

Google Play plans renew until you cancel them in Google Play; please cancel at least 48 hours before the renewal date. Plans paid to us directly do not renew by themselves.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, if you ask within 48 hours of paying, whether you paid on Google Play or to us directly. After 48 hours there is no refund. Full details are in the terms of service.

I live outside Pakistan. What do I pay?

This page shows the price for the country you are in. Write to us on WhatsApp or by email to arrange payment.
