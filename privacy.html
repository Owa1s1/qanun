Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026. This policy covers the Qanun apps for Android, Windows and Mac, and the website alqanun.pk.

In short: Qanun has no advertising and no analytics, and we never sell your information. Your books, notes and study work are kept on your own device. To run accounts, plans, the free daily questions, the built-in AI and question reports, our server keeps a small amount of information: your email and name, codes for your devices, your plan, and counts of what you have used. We do not keep the text you send to the AI. Text goes to other services only when you use a feature that needs them, such as a PubMed search.

Who we are

Qanun is made by Dr. Owais Nawaz Khan, Peshawar, Pakistan. For any question about this policy, write to support@alqanun.pk.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to Qanun is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your profile (the name you enter, your year or exam), settings and theme;
  • your practice answers, scores, bookmarks, flashcards, Avicenna's notes and your study history;
  • books you import, with your highlights and notes, and question banks you import;
  • research projects and any files you add to them;
  • your own AI key, if you choose to use one instead of Qanun's built-in AI.

Backups and exports are made only when you ask for them, and saved where you choose. Progress sync: when you sign in to your Qanun account on the web, or connect the Windows or Android app to it (Settings, Account and plan), your answers and scores on Qanun bank questions, bookmarks, flags, highlights, notes, Ask Avicenna notes, flashcards with their review schedule, and your answer and review history are copied to our server, linked to your account, so they appear on your other devices. A connected app holds its own sign-in token, which we store only in scrambled (hashed) form; you can disconnect it at any time. Deleting your account deletes this copy too. Your books and files are never synced; they stay on the device where you added them.

Your account

You sign in with Google, or with an email address. Sign-in is handled by Google (Firebase Authentication); we never see your Google password. Our server, run on Cloudflare, keeps:

  • your email address and name, and an account code from Google;
  • your plan, its end date and your price region (the country group your price belongs to);
  • the devices registered to your account: the type (phone, tablet or computer), a device code and the date it was added. The device code is worked out on your device by a one-way calculation, so it cannot be turned back into your device's own identifier;
  • how many free questions and AI requests have been used, per day or month, so the limits work.

We use this only to run your account, your plan and the limits, and to stop one plan being shared among many people.

Buying a plan

Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or bank details. Google sends our server a purchase code, the plan bought, its renewal or end date, and the country of the purchase, which sets your price region. We keep these with your account to confirm your plan.

Paying us directly (Easypaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, SadaPay or bank transfer). We keep a record of the payment (name, amount, date and transaction reference) and of the plan we gave you, as needed for our accounts.

Licence keys (Windows and Mac apps). We keep a record of each licence we issue: the name on the licence, the device code, the plan and its dates. When online, the app checks its licence with our server, sending the licence number, the device code and the app version; the server notes when that licence was last seen on that device.

The built-in AI (Ask Avicenna, Search and Research)

On the free plan, Avicenna’s answers to the free questions were written in advance and come with the app, so nothing is sent to any AI service when you open them. When you use the AI with a plan, your request goes through our server to an AI service: an OpenRouter model, or Google (Gemini) when OpenRouter is busy. What is sent is the text you type and the passages the feature needs, such as the question you are on or an extract from a book you chose. Our server checks that your device and plan are allowed, counts the request, and passes it on. It does not keep the text of your requests or the answers. The AI service handles the text under its own terms; we choose services that do not use it to train their models where that choice is offered.

If you use your own AI key instead (in Advanced settings), your request goes straight from your device to the service you chose: Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), Groq or OpenRouter, or Ollama on your own computer. Free versions of some services, such as Google's, may use the text to improve their products; please read their terms.

Never type patient names or other details that could identify a patient.

The website: free daily questions

The free questions on alqanun.pk need a Google sign-in. Our server keeps your account details (see above), a random code made by your browser to recognise it, and which free questions you were given each day and the answer you chose, so the daily limit works and questions do not repeat.

Reports and feedback

If you report a problem with a question, or mark questions as a reviewer, the app sends the report to our server when you are online: the question code, the problems you picked and your note, the answer you chose, the app version, the name in your Qanun profile and a random code for your copy of the app. We use reports only to correct the question bank. You can also send reports by email or WhatsApp instead.

Permissions

The Android app asks only for internet access. It opens files only when you pick them (to import a book or restore a backup), and saves files only when you ask (exports and backups).

Who can use Qanun

Qanun is for medical and dental students and doctors aged 17 or over. Many students begin medical or dental college at 17, so 17-year-olds may use Qanun with the agreement of a parent or guardian. Qanun is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 17. If you believe someone under 17 has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

Account, device and plan records are kept while your account is open. When you delete your account, we keep only payment records the law requires and a daily count of free questions per device code (no name or email), so the free allowance cannot be reset by making a new account. Daily free-question records and AI usage counts are kept only as long as the limits need them. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires for accounts. Reports are kept until the question has been corrected and checked.

Deleting your account and information

  • On your device: use Settings, Privacy and data, Delete my data, or uninstall the app. Copies you saved yourself (backups, exports) stay until you delete them.
  • Your account on our server: in the app, open Settings, Privacy and data, Delete my Qanun account; or go to alqanun.pk/practice.html#delete-account, sign in and confirm. Your account, devices, plan, usage counts, purchase records and served free questions are deleted at once. You can also write to support@alqanun.pk from your account's email, and we delete it within 30 days. Question reports can be deleted on request. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play first.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information our server holds about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to the email above. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights given by data protection law there, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
